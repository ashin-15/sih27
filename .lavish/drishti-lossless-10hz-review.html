The capacity limit

A queue buys time, not capacity

At a steady deficit, any finite disk or RAM store fills. Reaching 10 Hz throughput and meeting 100 ms capture-to-output latency are separate tests. A delayed map must be marked stale for live consumers.

Proposed architecture / recorder tested in isolation

Commit, compute, publish in order

The isolated SQLite WAL/FULL test committed and reverified 100 paced scans on ext4/NVMe; read-through-commit p99 was 22.05 ms. Runtime integration and failure recovery remain open. Shared memory saves copies but is volatile. The committed scan is the recovery source. Stateful stages keep frame order; only proven stateless work enters the worker pool.

Open product contract

Specify overload behavior

Sensor retry or flow control, disk reserve, maximum acceptable map age and live stop behavior are unresolved. No software can recover a scan that the sensor delivered but the recorder never committed.

No no-loss or 100 ms guarantee is claimed today.
