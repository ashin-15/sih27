# Product specification rules

Read the root and package `AGENTS.md`. The perception PRD, technical design, acceptance criteria,
and loop record here are drafts until their contract is frozen and explicitly approved. Keep
requirements, decisions, and measurable acceptance criteria linked by ID. Record unresolved
choices as TBD or BLOCKED with the needed decision. Do not mark product features implemented
from a design description, a synthetic run, or historical evidence. Update `LOOP.md` as the
specification or implementation status changes.
